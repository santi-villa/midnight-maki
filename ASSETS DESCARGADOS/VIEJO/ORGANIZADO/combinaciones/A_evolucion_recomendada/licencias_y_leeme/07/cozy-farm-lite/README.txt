Cozy Farm Lite — 16 x 16 top-down farming asset pack

Cozy pixel art for top-down farming and life-sim games on a 16 px grid.
285 PNG files (1x, 2x and 4x, nearest-neighbor, transparent background).
Includes a playable farmer walking in 4 directions (16 x 24, player-farmer/);
the full 8-animation sheet (idle, walk, run, carry, sit, water, hoe, harvest)
is in Cozy Farm Complete.

This is the free Lite version. The full Cozy Farm adds water with animated
ripples, 7 more crops (potato, corn, pumpkin, strawberry, cabbage, turnip,
sunflower), the farmhouse, barn and chicken coop, 7 more villagers, cows and a
brown hen, 3 more trees, 19 more items and 17 more props:
https://aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-farm

tiles/       ground tiles, 16 x 16: grass (plain and with flowers) and
             13-piece autotiles over grass for: soil, soil-wet, path.
             Autotile pieces: nw n ne / w c e / sw s se (outer edges) and
             inner-nw inner-ne inner-sw inner-se (inner corners). The edge is
             3 px wide on the grass side, so any rectangle or L shape fits.
             
tileset/     the same ground tiles and the fences on one sheet each (16 px grid)
fences/      16 x 16 fence pieces that connect: posts, horizontal and vertical
             rails, ends, corners, a cross, and a gate (closed and open)
crops/       carrot, tomato, wheat
             4 stages each: seeds, sprout, growing, ready. One PNG per stage
             and a 4-frame strip per crop (tall crops are 16 x 32).
items/       inventory icons, 16 x 16: carrot, tomato, wheat, seed-bag, watering-can, hoe
props/       hay-bale, crate, barrel, rock, bush, flowers
             Props sit on the bottom edge of a canvas sized in whole tiles.
trees/       oak (32 x 48)
buildings/   —
characters/  farmer
             3 x 4 sheet of 16 x 16 frames: rows walk down, left, right, up;
             columns step, stand, step (play 1-2-3-2 at ~8 fps).
animals/     chicken-white
             Same 3 x 4 walk layout (hens 16 x 16, cows 32 x 32) plus a 5th row:
             pecking / grazing facing right and left.
sheets/      every crop, item, prop, tree and building on one sheet per group

Made by hand in code and text-art (one letter per color), with a fixed
palette and a 1 px warm outline.
