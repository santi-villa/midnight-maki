COZY FARM — PLAYABLE FARMER (Update 1)
=======================================

One playable farmer, 16 x 24 frames, top-down 3/4 view (walk only in this free Lite file; the full 8-animation sheet —idle, walk, run, carry, sit, water, hoe and harvest— is in Cozy Farm Complete)

(same body and shading
engine as Cozy Town's characters; same world, same warm 1 px outline).

Files
-----
characters/1x, 2x, 4x/farmer.png   the sprite sheet (4 rows x 6 columns, 96 x 96 px at 1x)
characters/farmer.json             rows, frame counts, suggested fps and loop, for engines
gif/farmer-walk.gif                sample: walking in the 4 directions

Frame
-----
Every frame is 16 x 24 px. Feet rest on pixel row 21 (counting from 0 at
the top): offset y = -21 from the feet to align to a 16 px tile grid.
Left is the mirror of right, shading redrawn so light still comes from the left.

Row order
---------
  row  0-3   walk     6 frames  10 fps  loop

Columns run left to right; unused cells in shorter animations are transparent.
